Episode 5

The Interpreter’s House

Watch on YouTubeSubscribe

The big idea

God’s rules show us our sin, but only his grace can clean our hearts. His gifts are worth waiting for, and Jesus keeps our faith burning even when we can’t see him at work.

From the Bible

“I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you shall be clean.”Ezekiel 36:25 (ESV)
“Lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust destroys.”Matthew 6:20 (ESV)
“He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.”Philippians 1:6 (ESV)

What it means

The dusty room
The dust is sin hidden in the heart. Sweeping it (the Law) just stirs it up so you can see how much there is. Sprinkling water first (the good news of grace) is what settles it and makes the room clean. That’s why Christian couldn’t find rest at Mount Sinai.
Passion and Patience
Passion wants everything right now, and soon has nothing. Patience waits and receives treasure that lasts. Bunyan uses them to compare living only for today with trusting God’s promises for the future.
The fire and the hidden oil
The fire is God’s work of grace in a believer’s heart. The Devil keeps trying to put it out, but Christ secretly keeps pouring in oil, so it burns brighter. When faith feels weak, it may be that we can’t see the hand still feeding it.
The Interpreter
He explains what Christian sees. Bunyan often pictured the Holy Spirit this way: helping us understand God’s truth.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • What happened when the little mouse swept the dusty floor? What made it clean?
  • What did Passion want? What happened to all his treasures?
  • Why didn’t the fire go out when the dark figure poured water on it?
  • Who was pouring the oil?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • The broom only stirred up the dust. How do God’s rules show us our sin without being able to take it away?
  • Where do you see “Passion” thinking in everyday life (wanting things right now)? What helps us be patient?
  • Have you ever felt like your faith was going out? What does the hidden oil tell you about those times?
  • Why do you think Christ’s hand was hidden behind the wall?

Do it together

Try the dust lesson for real: sprinkle a little flour on a tray and brush it with a dry brush (it puffs up), then mist it with water first and wipe it (it cleans up). Talk about the difference between rules and grace.

Pray

Dear God, thank you that your grace cleans our hearts in a way our own trying never could. Help us wait for the treasures that last, and thank you that Jesus keeps our faith burning, even when we can’t see him. Amen.
