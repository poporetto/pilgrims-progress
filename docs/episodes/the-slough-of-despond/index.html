Episode 2

The Slough of Despond

Watch on YouTubeSubscribe

The big idea

Following Jesus doesn’t mean life is always easy. When we feel stuck, scared or discouraged, God doesn’t leave us there. He gives us his promises to stand on and sends help.

From the Bible

“He drew me up from the pit of destruction, out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure.”Psalm 40:2 (ESV)
“Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.”Isaiah 41:10 (ESV)

What it means

The Slough of Despond
“Despond” means losing hope. Help explains it: when someone first realises how much they need God, fears and doubts come rushing in and settle in this low place. Feeling discouraged doesn’t mean you’re on the wrong road.
The Steps
Solid stones run through the middle of the bog, but Christian was too frightened to look for them. In Bunyan’s book the steps are God’s promises, the things that hold firm when our feelings sink.
Pliable goes home
Pliable wanted crowns and adventure. When it got hard, he quit. Jesus describes this in the parable of the sower (Mark 4:16–17): some people receive the message with joy, but without roots they fall away when trouble comes.
Help
Help stands for the help God gives, very often through other people. Christian couldn’t climb out alone, and the story doesn’t pretend he could.
Still carrying the burden
Christian is rescued from the mud, but the burden is still on his back. Being helped through a hard time isn’t the same as being forgiven. That comes later, at the Cross.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • Why did Christian and Pliable fall into the mud?
  • How did Pliable feel when he was covered in mud? What did he decide to do?
  • Who helped Christian get out?
  • Have you ever felt stuck or sad? Who helped you?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • Pliable followed for the crowns and glory. Why do you think he gave up so quickly?
  • Help asked why Christian didn’t look for the Steps. What are some of God’s promises you could “stand on” when you feel low?
  • Who has been a “Help” to you? How could you be a Help to someone else this week?
  • Christian was out of the mud but still carrying his burden. What’s the difference between being helped and being forgiven?

Do it together

Make paper stepping stones and write one of God’s promises on each (try Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 40:2, Matthew 28:20, Hebrews 13:5). Lay them across the floor as a path over the “bog”. Your child crosses by reading each promise aloud as they step on it.

Pray

Dear God, when we feel stuck and sad, help us remember your promises. Thank you for the people you send to help us. Make us good helpers for others, and help us keep going with you. Amen.
