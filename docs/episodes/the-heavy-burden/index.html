Episode 1

The Heavy Burden

Watch on YouTubeSubscribe

The big idea

Everyone carries a burden of wrong things they have done, and we cannot take it off by ourselves. Jesus invites us to come to him.

From the Bible

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”Matthew 11:28 (ESV)
“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”Psalm 119:105 (ESV)
“I am the door. If anyone enters by me, he will be saved.”John 10:9 (ESV)

What it means

The burden
The heavy pack stands for sin: the wrong things we think, say and do, and the guilt that comes with them. Christian tries, but he cannot take it off himself. That is the whole problem of the story.
The book
Christian learns about his burden by reading a book. In Bunyan’s story it is the Bible, which shows us both our need and God’s way out.
Evangelist
His name means someone who brings good news. He doesn’t carry Christian; he points him to the way. God often uses people to show us where to look.
The shining light and the Wicket Gate
Christian can’t see the gate yet, but he can see the light, so he walks toward what he can see. The light is God’s Word; the narrow gate is Jesus himself, the way in (John 10:9).
Obstinate and Pliable
Two ways people respond. Obstinate refuses and mocks. Pliable gets excited about golden crowns, but he hasn’t thought about what following will cost. Episode 2 shows what happens to him.
Leaving home
In the book, Christian’s wife Christiana and their children follow him later (that is Part Two). Following Jesus is about putting him first, not abandoning the people we love. Christian begs his family to come.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • What was on Christian’s back? How do you think it felt to carry it every day?
  • Who helped Christian know which way to go?
  • Christian couldn’t see the gate. What could he see?
  • What did Obstinate do? What did Pliable do?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • What do you think Christian’s burden stands for? Why couldn’t he take it off himself?
  • Have you ever felt “heavy” inside after doing something wrong? What helped?
  • Obstinate wanted Christian to stay where everything was familiar. Why is it hard to choose differently from your friends?
  • Christian walked toward the light before he could see the gate. What does that teach about trusting God one step at a time?

Do it together

Put a few heavy books or stones in a backpack. Let your child wear it while you talk: each one is like a wrong thing we’ve done. Ask how it feels after a minute. Then take it off together and talk about Jesus inviting us to bring our burdens to him. Tell them the story will show where Christian’s burden finally falls off.

Pray

Dear God, thank you that you see the heavy things we carry. Thank you for sending Jesus to be the way to you. Help us to keep our eyes on your light and follow you, even when others don’t. Amen.
