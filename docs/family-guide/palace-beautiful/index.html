Chapter VIII

Palace Beautiful

Two lions roar beside the road, but they are chained. Christian keeps to the middle and is welcomed into a warm house, where friends feed him, encourage him and give him armor.

The big idea

God gives us other believers to welcome, teach and strengthen us, and he gives us what we need to stand firm.

From the Bible

“Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.”Ephesians 6:11 (ESV)
“Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.”Isaiah 41:10 (ESV)

What it means

The chained lions
Some fears are loud, but they can go no further than God allows.
The house
The church: a family where pilgrims are cared for.
The armor
Salvation, righteousness, faith and God’s Word. All of it comes from the King.
The view
A glimpse of where the road is going, to give him courage.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • Why couldn’t the lions hurt Christian?
  • Who looked after him in the house?
  • Which piece of armor would you pick first?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • What fears in your life sound louder than they really are?
  • Who helps you follow Jesus?
  • Why do you think the armor came before the next valley?

Do it together

Make cardboard armor and label each piece: helmet (salvation), breastplate (righteousness), shield (faith), sword (God’s Word).

Pray

Dear God, thank you for our church family and for your armor. Help us be brave and stay on your path. Amen.
