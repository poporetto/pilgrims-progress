Chapter VII

Hill Difficulty

Christian climbs the steep path while two travellers take easy ones. He falls asleep, loses his scroll, and has to go back for it. At the top, others run away from lions, but he goes on.

The big idea

The right way is sometimes the hard way. When we slip up, we can go back, put it right, and keep going.

From the Bible

“Let us not grow weary of doing good, for in due season we will reap, if we do not give up.”Galatians 6:9 (ESV)
“When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.”Psalm 56:3 (ESV)

What it means

The steep path
Following Jesus can be hard work, and it leads to life.
The arbor
Rest is a gift from God. Christian’s trouble came from forgetting why he was there.
The lost scroll
Losing sight of God’s promise. He found it where he had let go of it.
Timorous and Mistrust
Fear that makes people turn back.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • Which path did Christian choose?
  • What did he lose while he slept?
  • What scared the two runners?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • When have you had to go back and put something right?
  • What is the difference between resting and giving up?
  • Christian was scared and still went on. What helped him?

Do it together

Climb some stairs or a hill together. At the top, say one hard thing you want to keep doing this week.

Pray

Dear God, give us strength when the path is steep. When we make mistakes, help us go back and begin again. Amen.
