Wicket Gate & the Interpreter

Goodwill pulls Christian safely through the gate, away from flying arrows. Then the Interpreter, a wise owl, shows him six sights to remember.

Guide for Episode 4: The Wicket Gate

The big idea

Jesus is the way in, and he welcomes everyone who comes. Then God patiently teaches us to understand.

From the Bible

“I am the door. If anyone enters by me, he will be saved.”John 10:9 (ESV)
“Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.”Matthew 7:7 (ESV)

What it means

The narrow gate
Jesus himself, the only way onto the King’s road.
The dust and the water
Rules stir up the dust of sin. The good news of grace is what washes the heart clean.
The fire and the oil
The enemy pours water on our faith, but Christ quietly keeps it burning.
The knight, the cage and the dream
Be brave, keep a soft heart, and live ready to meet the King.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • What happened when Christian knocked?
  • Why didn’t the fire go out?
  • Which room was your favourite?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • Passion wanted everything now. When is it hard for you to wait?
  • What helps keep our hearts soft toward God?
  • Why do you think Goodwill pulled Christian in so quickly?

Do it together

Draw six small doors. Behind each one, draw a thing the Interpreter showed Christian and one thing it teaches.

Pray

Dear Jesus, thank you for opening the door to everyone who knocks. Teach us, and keep our faith burning. Amen.
