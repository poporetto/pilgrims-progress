Chapter VI

The King’s Highway

Christian tries to wake three sleepers in chains, but they won’t listen. Then Formalist and Hypocrisy climb over the wall instead of coming through the gate.

The big idea

Looking like a pilgrim isn’t the same as knowing the King. We come to God his way, through Jesus.

From the Bible

“Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.”Ephesians 5:14 (ESV)
“I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”John 14:6 (ESV)

What it means

Simple, Sloth and Presumption
Not seeing the danger, not wanting to bother, and thinking we will be fine without help.
The wall
Trying to reach God by custom or good behaviour instead of through Jesus.
The scroll
Christian’s hope isn’t that he looks right, but that the King gave him a promise.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • Why wouldn’t the sleepers wake up?
  • How did the two travellers get onto the road?
  • What did Christian have that they didn’t?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • Why is it hard to help someone who doesn’t want help?
  • What is the difference between acting like a Christian and trusting Jesus?
  • What would it look like to follow someone’s customs but not their words?

Do it together

Play “sleepy pilgrims”: one person pretends to sleep while the other tries to wake them kindly. Talk about caring for friends who don’t listen yet.

Pray

Dear God, wake us up to what matters. Help us trust Jesus for real, not only look as if we do. Amen.
