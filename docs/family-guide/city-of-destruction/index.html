Guide for Episode 1: The Heavy Burden

The big idea

We all carry wrong things we have done, and we can’t take that weight off by ourselves. Jesus invites us to come to him.

From the Bible

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”Matthew 11:28 (ESV)
“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”Psalm 119:105 (ESV)

What it means

The burden
Sin, and the guilt that comes with it. Christian can’t lift it off himself.
Evangelist
His name means someone who brings good news. He points Christian the right way.
Obstinate and Pliable
One refuses to listen. The other comes along because it sounds exciting, without thinking about how hard it may get.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • What was on Christian’s back?
  • Who showed him the way to go?
  • What could Christian see in the distance?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • Why couldn’t Christian take the burden off himself?
  • Have you ever felt heavy inside after doing something wrong? What helped?
  • Why is it hard to choose differently from your friends?

Do it together

Put some books in a backpack and wear it while you talk. How does it feel after a minute? Take it off together and talk about giving our burdens to Jesus.

Pray

Dear God, thank you that you see the heavy things we carry. Help us to come to Jesus and follow your light. Amen.
