Chapter XV

Enchanted Ground

A flatterer in shining clothes traps the pilgrims, Atheist laughs at them, and sleepy fog tries to make them stop. They stay awake by talking, remembering and singing. Ignorance trusts his own goodness.

The big idea

Check what people say against God’s Word, and help each other stay awake to him.

From the Bible

“So then let us not sleep, as others do, but let us keep awake and be sober.”1 Thessalonians 5:6 (ESV)
“For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God.”Ephesians 2:8 (ESV)

What it means

The Flatterer
Someone who praises us and looks good, but leads us the wrong way.
Atheist
Being laughed at doesn’t make God’s promise untrue.
The sleepy ground
Slowly losing interest in God.
Ignorance
Trusting that being a good person is enough.

Questions for ages 4–7

  • Why did they get caught in the net?
  • How did the friends stay awake?
  • What did Ignorance trust?

Questions for ages 8–12

  • How can you test whether a teacher is telling the truth?
  • What makes people sleepy toward God, and what wakes them up?
  • How is trusting God’s kindness different from trusting our own goodness?

Do it together

Sit in a circle and take turns finishing: “I remember when God helped me…” Then sing a favourite song together.

Pray

Dear God, keep us awake. Help us know your Word, and help us to encourage one another. Amen.
