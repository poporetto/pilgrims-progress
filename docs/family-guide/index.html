Pilgrim’s Progress Family Guide

Talk about the story together. Every chapter has its meaning, Bible verses, questions for two age groups, something to do and a short prayer.

  1. ICity of DestructionChristian says goodbye to his family and leaves home with a heavy burden. Evangelist points him to the light, and his neighbours Obstinate and Pliable chase after him.
  2. IISlough of DespondChristian and Pliable tumble into a muddy bog. Pliable gives up and goes home, but Help reaches down and pulls Christian out.
  3. IIIWorldly Wiseman & Mount SinaiMr. Worldly Wiseman talks Christian into an easier way, but it leads under a fiery, frightening mountain. Evangelist finds him and sends him back to the true road.
  4. IVWicket Gate & the InterpreterGoodwill pulls Christian safely through the gate, away from flying arrows. Then the Interpreter, a wise owl, shows him six sights to remember.
  5. VThe CrossOn a green hill, Christian looks up at the Cross. His burden falls off and rolls into the empty tomb. Three Shining Ones bring him forgiveness, new clothes and a sealed scroll.
  6. VIThe King’s HighwayChristian tries to wake three sleepers in chains, but they won’t listen. Then Formalist and Hypocrisy climb over the wall instead of coming through the gate.
  7. VIIHill DifficultyChristian climbs the steep path while two travellers take easy ones. He falls asleep, loses his scroll, and has to go back for it. At the top, others run away from lions, but he goes on.
  8. VIIIPalace BeautifulTwo lions roar beside the road, but they are chained. Christian keeps to the middle and is welcomed into a warm house, where friends feed him, encourage him and give him armor.
  9. IXValley of HumiliationApollyon blocks the road, reminds Christian of every mistake and attacks him. Christian falls, then takes up his sword and stands again.
  10. XShadow of DeathChristian walks a narrow path through the dark, praying when he can’t see. Morning comes, and he finds a friend: Faithful, whose voice gave him courage in the night.
  11. XIVanity FairTalkative talks well but hasn’t changed. At Vanity Fair everything is for sale, and the pilgrims are arrested because they only want the truth. Faithful is killed for trusting the King, who takes him home. Hopeful joins Christian.
  12. XIIHill LucreBy-ends follows faith only when it is comfortable. Demas calls everyone to a glittering silver mine, and those who turn aside are lost. A pillar of salt reminds the pilgrims not to look back.
  13. XIIIDoubting CastleChristian and Hopeful leave the rocky road for a soft meadow and are locked up by Giant Despair. At last Christian remembers the key called Promise, and it opens every lock.
  14. XIVDelectable MountainsFour shepherds feed the pilgrims, show them some dangers to avoid, and let them look through a glass at the Celestial City.
  15. XVEnchanted GroundA flatterer in shining clothes traps the pilgrims, Atheist laughs at them, and sleepy fog tries to make them stop. They stay awake by talking, remembering and singing. Ignorance trusts his own goodness.
  16. XVIBeulah LandIn a golden land in sight of the City, the pilgrims come to the deep River. Christian is afraid and begins to sink, but Hopeful holds him up, and together they reach the other side.
  17. XVIIThe Celestial CityAngels lead Christian and Hopeful up to the gates. Faithful greets them, their scrolls are accepted, and they are welcomed home to the King. Ignorance reaches the gate without the King’s promise and is turned away.
