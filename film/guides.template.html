Pilgrim's Progress Family Guides

Each short episode follows Christian the bear on his journey to the Celestial City, from John Bunyan's classic allegory about following Jesus. For every episode you'll find what the story means, Bible verses, questions for two age groups, a family activity, a prayer, and a video description ready to paste.
