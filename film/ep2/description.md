Pilgrim's Progress, Episode 2: The Slough of Despond

Christian and Pliable are so busy dreaming about the golden city that they fall straight into a deep, muddy bog. Pliable gives up and goes home, but Christian keeps struggling forward, until a kind helper named Help pulls him out.

Following Jesus doesn’t mean life is always easy. When we feel stuck, scared or discouraged, God doesn’t leave us there. He gives us his promises to stand on and sends help.

“He drew me up from the pit of destruction, out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure.” (Psalm 40:2, ESV)
