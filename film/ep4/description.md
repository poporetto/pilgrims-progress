Pilgrim's Progress, Episode 4: The Wicket Gate

Worried that he’ll be turned away, Christian finally reaches the little gate and knocks. Goodwill, a great golden lion, throws it open, pulls him inside just as arrows fly from a dark castle, and welcomes him onto the King’s Highway.

Jesus welcomes everyone who comes to him, no matter how muddy their story is. We don’t have to clean ourselves up first. We just knock.

“Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.” (Matthew 7:7, ESV)
