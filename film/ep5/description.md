Pilgrim's Progress, Episode 5: The Interpreter’s House

A wise old owl called the Interpreter shows Christian three strange rooms: a dusty floor that sweeping only makes worse, two little bears named Passion and Patience, and a fire that keeps burning no matter how much water is poured on it.

God’s rules show us our sin, but only his grace can clean our hearts. His gifts are worth waiting for, and Jesus keeps our faith burning even when we can’t see him at work.

“I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you shall be clean.” (Ezekiel 36:25, ESV)
