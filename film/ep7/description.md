Pilgrim's Progress, Episode 7: The Cross

Christian climbs a sunny green hill with his heavy burden. He looks up at the Cross, the straps come loose, and the burden rolls down into the empty tomb. Three Shining Ones bring him forgiveness, new clothes and a sealed scroll, and he goes on his way singing.

Jesus carried our sin for us on the cross and rose again. Forgiveness is a gift we receive, not something we earn.

“He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree.” (1 Peter 2:24, ESV)
