Pilgrim's Progress, Episode 1: The Heavy Burden

Christian the bear carries a heavy burden he cannot take off. A kind stranger named Evangelist points him toward a shining light, and Christian runs for the gate, even when his neighbours chase after him.

Everyone carries a burden of wrong things they have done, and we cannot take it off by ourselves. Jesus invites us to come to him.

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.” (Matthew 11:28, ESV)
