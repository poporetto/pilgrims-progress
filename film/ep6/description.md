Pilgrim's Progress, Episode 6: The Knight, the Cage and the Dream

In the last rooms of the Interpreter’s House, Christian watches a brave knight fight his way into a shining palace, meets a sad man locked in an iron cage, and hears about a dream that made a man tremble. Then he sets off down the road again.

Following Jesus takes courage, and it is worth it. Keep your heart soft toward God, and when you go wrong, run straight back to him. Live each day ready to meet the King, with joy instead of fear.

“Through many tribulations we must enter the kingdom of God.” (Acts 14:22, ESV)
